## The responsibility

At RestoreAI, I own the infrastructure around the product and how it is deployed. The work covers production, staging, development, and preview workflows, alongside GPU infrastructure and model serving.

## What I contribute

- Provision and manage cloud infrastructure with Terraform and Terragrunt.
- Automate deployment workflows so the team can build, release, and investigate failed deployments across environments.
- Operate GPU infrastructure and NVIDIA NIM/VSS workloads, including model runtime testing and GPU sizing against workload requirements.
- Improve production access using SSM and Tailscale.
- Lead technical SOC 2 Type II readiness and automate evidence collection. This is preparation work, not a claim of completed certification.
- Review infrastructure costs and remove unused services, including an unused Microsoft Entra service costing $300/month.

## The outcome

The team has repeatable deployment workflows and a named owner for infrastructure operations, access, and recovery. GPU and model-serving work is part of that platform responsibility, rather than a separate claim about training AI models or owning application behavior.

The work is ongoing. This public summary covers my scope and contributions without exposing internal architecture or access details.

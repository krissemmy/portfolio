## The problem

Dailzero needed to move its infrastructure and give the founder a clear way to operate the result. The database migration also had to preserve PostgreSQL extension compatibility and existing application data.

## My contribution

I handled the move to a Hetzner and Dokploy setup with production and staging environments, including the migration from Neon to PostgreSQL with pgvector.

Before the database cutover, I checked version and extension compatibility, adapted the application's database connection, and rehearsed a dump and restore. The checks covered row counts, foreign keys, vector data, and migration history.

I configured scheduled database backups to Cloudflare R2 and verified a restore into the staging environment. I also documented deployment, rollback, backup, and recovery procedures for the founder.

## What was verified

The August 2026 handover records healthy application endpoints, scheduled backups writing to R2, and a successful staging restore with data and expected database structures present.

That restore was tested in staging. It is not evidence of a production restore or a complete disaster-recovery exercise. The case study makes no quantified savings or measured downtime claim.

## The tradeoff

Moving from managed services transfers maintenance and recovery work to the team. The handover therefore covered how to operate the system and distinguish an application rollback from a database restore, not just how to deploy it once.
